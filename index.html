Before you go

What actually needs doing

Schengen visa

Apply through France as country of first entry. VFS Seattle appointments book out weeks ahead — the one item that can end the trip before it starts.

Blocking

Re-entry documents

I-20 travel signature current within six months, EAD card, and an employment verification letter in your carry-on, not checked.

Money

One ticket, three legs

Search multi-city: SEA→CDG on the 3rd, BUD→CDG on the 12th, CDG→SEA on the 13th. On one itinerary, a Budapest delay becomes the airline's problem.

Free time

Trim the mornings

Morning routine is the largest slice of dead time. Thirty minutes saved off each buys back four hours — more than a whole Vienna stopover.

Hotels

Stay near the stations

Local transfers cost nearly as much as all intra-Europe train travel. Walking distance to Gare du Nord, Centraal, Praha hlavní and Keleti is the only real fix.
