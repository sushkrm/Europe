Before you go

What actually needs doing

Blocking

Schengen visa

Through the French consulate — France wins on both tests, longest stay and first entry. Apply via TLScontact, not VFS: the Seattle centre is at 1420 Fifth Avenue, applications are adjudicated in San Francisco, and it's 15 working days minimum with no expedite option.

Blocking

Entry biometrics

The EU's Entry/Exit System has been live since April 2026 — budget extra time for biometrics on first entry at CDG, on top of the usual immigration queue.

Blocking

Re-entry documents

I-20 travel signature current within six months, EAD card, and an employment verification letter in your carry-on, not checked.

Money

One ticket, three legs

Search multi-city: SEA→CDG on the 3rd, CDG→SEA on the 13th, plus the Amsterdam→Budapest and Budapest→CDG hops. Confirm what's carry-on only before booking the tightest connections.

Free time

Trim the mornings

Morning routine is the largest slice of dead time on this plan. Thirty minutes saved off each buys back several hours across ten days.

Hotels

Stay near the stations

Four bases this time, not six — Paris, Amsterdam, Budapest, CDG. Walking distance to Gare du Nord, Centraal and Keleti is still the only real fix for local-transfer time.
